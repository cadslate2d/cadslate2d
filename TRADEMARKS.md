# Trademark policy

CadSlate2D's code is free software under the AGPL ([`LICENSE`](LICENSE)): anyone may use, study, change, and share it. The licence grants the code, not the name. This policy says how the name and the logo may be used. Its aim is that people can always tell the official CadSlate2D, and its AI service, from a version someone else has changed.

## The marks

- The name **CadSlate2D**.
- The logo, [`cadslate2d_logo.png`](cadslate2d_logo.png), and its variants.

They belong to the project's maintainer, Leonardo Araujo. They are not registered yet; a registration is planned (#293), and until then they are used as unregistered trademarks.

## What is official

- **The code:** this repository, <https://github.com/cadslate2d/cadslate2d>.
- **The application:** the builds the maintainer publishes from it, at the address the README gives.
- **The AI service:** the hosted service the official application signs in to, run by the maintainer. Nobody else's service is the CadSlate2D AI service.

## What you may do without asking

- **Name the project truthfully:**
  - say that your work is "based on CadSlate2D", "a fork of CadSlate2D", or "compatible with CadSlate2D";
  - write about it, review it, teach it, and link to it.
- **Share it unchanged:** pass on or host an unmodified official build under its name, as long as you do not suggest that you are the project or that it endorses you. A hosted copy should say plainly that it is not the official site.
- **Contribute:** use the name in issues, pull requests, and discussion of the project.

## What needs a name of your own

- **A modified version.** If you change the code and share or host the result, give it a name of your own and remove the logo. You may say it is based on CadSlate2D. The AGPL lets you do this, and asks you to offer your users its source (§13). It does not let you use the name, which AGPL §7(e) allows a licensor to withhold.
- **A product or service of your own,** including an AI service the application signs in to, an app-store listing, or a hosted offering, under the name or the logo.
- **A confusingly similar name or logo:** "CadSlate", "CadSlate 2D Pro", or the logo recoloured.
- **A domain name, organisation, or account** using the name, unless it is clearly about the project rather than speaking for it, such as a user group or a fan site that says so.
- **Merchandise** bearing the name or the logo, for sale.

## Asking

To ask for a use this policy does not cover, open an issue in this repository and title it "Trademark:". Uses the maintainer allows are written down, and this policy may grow from them.
