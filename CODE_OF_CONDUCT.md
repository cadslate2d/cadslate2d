# Code of conduct

CadSlate2D is a small project. Its issues and pull requests are a workplace, and the standard is the one a good workshop keeps.

- **Be straightforward.** Say what is wrong and why, with evidence: the commands, the file, the test. Disagree with reasons, in the issue.
- **Be kind.** No insults, harassment, or contempt, of anyone, for any reason. Assume the other person is trying to make the program better too.
- **Stay on the work.** Personal attributes, politics, and off-topic argument do not belong here.
- **Respect decisions.** The design documents and the ADRs record what was decided and why. To change one, write the case, as an ADR; do not relitigate it in every thread.

The maintainer enforces this. A first problem gets a word; a repeated or serious one gets the person removed from the project's spaces. To report a problem, write to the maintainer privately through GitHub.
