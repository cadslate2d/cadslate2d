# ADR-0031: A construction line is an entity without bounds, kept beside the index and drawn clipped to the view

**Status:** Accepted (2026-10-14)  
**Date:** 2026-10-14  
**Kind:** Design (#206)  
**Design refs:** §12, §18, §19; ADR-0003, ADR-0009, ADR-0012, ADR-0022, ADR-0027

## Context

A drafter lays out with lines that go on for ever: `XLINE` both ways, `RAY` one way. Everything else in the drawing has bounds, and three things rest on that: the spatial index (an R-tree of boxes), the extents (`ZOOM Extents`, the plot's fit), and the committed scene, which is packed once, in `f32` offsets from a chunk origin, and never regenerated by pan or zoom (ADR-0003, ADR-0022, ADR-0028). An endless line has no box, no place in the extents, and no finite segment the scene could pack.

## Decision

1. **An entity like the others.** `XLineEntity` (a base point and a unit direction) and `RayEntity` (a start and a unit direction) are `Entity` variants: in transactions and the one history, on layers with properties, picked, selected by a crossing window (never by a window, which cannot hold one), moved, turned, scaled, mirrored, stretched by their base point, offset, trimmed, broken, written to and read from DXF (`XLINE`, `RAY`).
2. **Kept beside the index, not in it.** `Drawing` keeps the IDs of the entities without bounds in a side list; every query returns the tree's candidates and those of the side list whose line crosses the box, tested one by one. The tree never holds a box two million units wide, so its other queries keep their shape. `extents` leaves them out, so `ZOOM Extents` and a plot's fit are the rest of the drawing's.
3. **A reach for geometry.** Every geometric question reads a construction line as a segment `CONSTRUCTION_REACH` — a million units — each way from its base (one way for a ray): primitives, crossings with a window, trimming, breaking. Where precision matters it is read locally instead: a crossing with another entity is computed with the line clipped to that entity's bounds, two construction lines meet by an exact formula, and the snaps (nearest, perpendicular) read it clipped to the view. So a crossing is found at the other entity's scale, not at the reach's.
4. **Drawn clipped to the view, in the overlay.** The committed scene holds none of it: `walk_entity` draws nothing for one. The overlay, rebuilt on every view change already, clips each shown construction line of the space to the view and draws the piece — as it draws far curves (ADR-0009) and the grid. Highlighted, hovered, or previewed, it is clipped the same way. A plot clips it to the plotted area, or to what a viewport shows of the model, and draws it with its full look.
5. **What it lacks on screen.** The overlay has no style table, so on screen a construction line is drawn in the theme's entity colour at the ordinary width, continuous — exactly what ADR-0009 accepted for far curves. On the page it has its colour, lineweight, and linetype.
6. **Trimming and breaking make lines and rays.** Cut by edges, the stretch at the pick goes: what is left of an xline is one or two rays, of a ray a line and perhaps a ray; broken, the same. A trim that turns a ray into a line moves its entry from the side list into the tree.
7. **Snaps.** An xline offers its base point as its midpoint, a ray its start as its endpoint; nearest, perpendicular, and intersection find them anywhere along their length. The ends of the reach are never offered.

## Evidence

Measured on the 100k mixed drawing (`benches/benches/construction.rs`, `cargo bench -p cad-benchmarks --bench construction`, this machine, 2026-10-14), with no construction lines and with a hundred spread across the extents:

| | None | 100 construction lines |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overlay rebuild at extents (every pointer move) | 25.25 ms ± 0.22 | 25.46 ms ± 0.30 |
| A pick or snap query, 10 px aperture | 1.40 µs | 4.35 µs |

A hundred construction lines add a fifth of a millisecond to an overlay rebuild — inside its noise — and three microseconds to a query. The overlay rebuild itself turned out to cost 25 ms at the extents of a 100k drawing with nothing in it: that is the far-curve pass of ADR-0009 reading every entity in view, found by this benchmark and filed as #210.

Precision: drawn in the overlay, a construction line's piece is computed in `f64` relative to the view centre at every frame, so it is placed to the pixel at any zoom — `a_construction_line_is_overlay_clipped_to_the_view_and_never_committed` pans a million units along one. Snapped crossings are computed locally: `construction_lines_snap_at_their_base_nearest_perpendicular_and_crossings` lands within 10⁻⁹ of the exact point.

## Alternatives considered

- **A committed segment the length of the extents plus the view, re-packed when the view leaves it.** Coloured like the rest — but the segment's ends are where its `f32` offsets are coarsest, and the line's position in between is interpolated from them: a segment a few drawing-widths long is placed to a pixel only at modest zoom, the limit #151 met for lines. And a view leaving the reach would regenerate committed geometry on pan, the one thing ADR-0003 forbids; a chunk re-pack costs 0.30 ms at 100k (ADR-0022), cheap, but on pan.
- **An infinite box in the tree.** The R-tree's nodes would take the line's box as theirs, and every query of every region would descend into them.
- **A `Primitive::Line` and `Primitive::Ray` in the geometry.** Exact everywhere, but every operation over primitives — intersection, offset, trim, extend, fillet, the tessellation of fills — would grow two arms for a case the reach and the local reading already answer.
- **A style table for the overlay**, giving construction lines and far curves their colours on screen: the right next step if drafters ask for coloured construction lines; not taken now, since it changes the overlay's packing and the adapter for both.

## Consequences

- `XLINE` (`XL`: through points, `Hor`, `Ver`, `Ang`, `Bisect`, `Offset` by a distance or through a point) and `RAY` draw them; `TRIM`, `BREAK`, `OFFSET`, `MOVE` and the rest edit them; `EXTEND`, `FILLET`, `CHAMFER`, `JOIN`, `LENGTHEN` refuse them by name.
- A construction line counts nowhere in the extents: a drawing of only construction lines has none, and `ZOOM Extents` leaves the view where it is.
- Geometric questions more than a million units from a construction line's base point find nothing there.

## Revisit when

- Drafters want their construction lines coloured on screen: a style table for the overlay, shared with far curves.
- A drawing needs construction lines whose crossings lie more than a million units from their base points.
